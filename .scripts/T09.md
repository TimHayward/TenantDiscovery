# T09: Per-tab URL routing and deep links

Read `CONTRACT.md` first. Wave 3, after wave 2 has merged. Estimated 3 hours.

## Objective

The dashboard's sixteen tabs are React state. Wouter is installed and routes exactly one path. Section deep-linking works through a window `CustomEvent` called `m365:open-section`. Replace all of it with real routing.

This is backlog 6.3.

## Why this matters more than it looks

This is a pre-sales and assessment tool. The output of using it is a conversation with a client about a specific finding. Right now you cannot send someone a link to a finding. You can send them a link to the dashboard and a sentence explaining which tab to click and which section to expand. Every report this tool produces is weaker for that.

The `CustomEvent` mechanism is also the kind of thing that works until two components listen for it.

## Work

### 1. Route the tabs

`/tab/:tab` with an optional `#section` fragment. Reloading `/tab/intune` must land on the Intune tab. Browser back and forward must walk tab history. `/` must redirect to the default tab, or render it, and you should pick one and say which.

Define the tab identifiers in one place, in `tabRoutes.ts`, and derive both the router and the tab list from it. Two lists of tab names that have to agree is the defect this task should not introduce.

Handle the unknown-tab case. `/tab/nonsense` should redirect to the default rather than rendering an empty shell.

### 2. Preserve the keep-alive semantics

`visitedTabs` mount-gating exists so that switching away from a tab and back does not refetch and remount everything. Routing naively will destroy that, and the regression will look like "the dashboard got slower" rather than like a bug.

Read how `visitedTabs` works now. Preserve the behaviour exactly. Prove it in verification.

### 3. Replace the CustomEvent

`Dashboard.tsx` dispatches `m365:open-section` and `CollapsibleSection.tsx` listens for it. Replace with navigation to `/tab/:tab#section`, with `CollapsibleSection` reading the fragment to decide whether to open and scroll.

Cross-tab links must still work: clicking a link on the Overview tab that points at a section on the Intune tab must switch tab, expand the section and scroll to it.

Remove the event listener and the dispatch once nothing uses them. Grep to confirm before removing.

### 4. Do not lose the lazy loading

Every tab is a `React.lazy` chunk. Routing must not convert them into eager imports, which is easy to do accidentally by importing the component into the route table. Verify by building and comparing chunk output.
